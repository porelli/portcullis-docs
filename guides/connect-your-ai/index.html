Connect Your AI¶

Use your Portcullis apps from Claude, Claude Code or another AI assistant, as yourself. The AI signs in with your passkey, so it can see and change only what you can, and deleting things always asks for your passkey in your browser. This page is for the people using it; administrators set it up in AI support.

You find everything on people.<domain> → My apps → Connect your AI. The card appears only while your administrator has AI support on and you can use at least one app (or your Portcullis account) through it.

What the card shows¶

  • All my apps: one address, https://mcp.<domain>/mcp, for every app you can use with AI, plus your Portcullis account. "Covers …" lists the apps it includes; apps added later join it on their own. Apps that sign you in themselves (Home Assistant) are connected separately.
  • One app only: each app's own address, its mode (read and write or read only, your administrator's choice) and a community mark when the app's MCP server isn't the app project's own. Steps opens the instructions for that app alone.
  • Portcullis (administrators): only for administrators, while the admin MCP is enabled for at least one client: https://hub.<domain>/mcp.
  • Your AI connections and Recent AI activity (below).

Each address has a Copy button, and the instructions come in tabs, one per AI client your administrator offers.

Claude desktop, claude.ai and Claude mobile¶

  1. Open Claude → Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector. (Team or Enterprise plan: ask an Owner to add it in Organization settings → Connectors → Add → Custom → Web, then click Connect.)
  2. Name: Portcullis apps (or the app's name), URL: the address from the card.
  3. Open Advanced settings (or choose "Use your own OAuth client"): OAuth Client ID portcullis-ai-claude. Leave the client secret empty.
  4. Click Add, then Connect. Sign in with your passkey and allow access.

Claude connects from Anthropic's servers, so this works only while your Portcullis is reachable from the internet.

Claude Code¶

Paste the command from the card in a terminal, for example:

claude mcp add --transport http --scope user --client-id portcullis-ai-claude-code \
  --callback-port 33418 portcullis-apps https://mcp.example.com/mcp

Then start Claude Code, type /mcp, choose portcullis-apps and sign in with your passkey in the browser that opens. Keep --callback-port 33418: the sign-in only accepts that port.

For a single app the server name is the app's name as the card shows it, lowercased, with other characters turned into - (gitea). When two rows would share a name, each gets its instance id added, then the companion name if that is still not enough (two Gitea instances: gitea-gitea and gitea-gitea-b).

Other MCP clients¶

Server URL: the card's address · Transport: Streamable HTTP · OAuth: the authorization server is discovered automatically (Keycloak at auth.<domain>) · Client ID portcullis-ai-generic · no secret · PKCE S256 · redirect http://localhost:33418/callback · scope mcp:all for All my apps (the card shows each app's own scope).

Apps that sign you in themselves¶

Some apps (Home Assistant) are their own sign-in server for AI clients. The card says "signs in with ":

  • Claude: add it as a custom connector with "Use Claude's published identity"; the app asks you to sign in (passkey).
  • Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http --scope user <name> <url>, without a client id.

The app applies its own rules: Portcullis doesn't log or confirm its AI actions.

When the AI wants to delete something¶

Deleting, and a few other risky actions, need you in your browser. The AI gives you a link (or your client opens it):

  1. Check the action, the target and the arguments: this is what will run, exactly. Values taken from the request are shown in quotes, and hidden characters (invisible or text-reversing ones) appear as \u… codes: decline if you see any you didn't expect.
  2. Type the check the page asks for (the app's label, such as Office for Nextcloud · Office, or for administrators the instance id or the person's name).
  3. Approve with your passkey, then ask the AI to try again.

A confirmation is valid for 10 minutes and works once; Decline needs no passkey. If the AI changes anything about the request, it needs a new one.

Your connections and activity¶

  • Each AI client you connected, when you connected it, when you last used it and until when the connection lasts. After that you sign in again in your assistant.
  • Disconnect ends all your connectors of that client at once (all your Claude connectors share one client). New sign-ins and token refreshes stop immediately; requests already signed in can keep working for up to 5 minutes. To remove one connector only, use Claude's own Remove (or claude mcp logout).
  • Recent AI activity: your last requests, as the tool names your AI used (gitea__list_issues ✓), with ⏸ waiting for your confirmation or ✗ and the reason when something was refused. Never the contents.

Good to know¶

  • What the AI reads in your apps is sent to the AI provider you use.
  • You get exactly your own access: leave a group and the AI loses that app at your next request.
  • On an AI page your administrator runs, "All my apps" is already the AI's only server: connect it once there and consent in the sign-in screen.
